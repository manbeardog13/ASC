## Drift audit and unresolved junction

The original combined upper-floor reference has a common front and rear edge
for both attics. The first procedural model incorrectly started the rack attic
at the office's south wall, shortening it to force a join to the existing house.
That is a reconstruction error, not a rotation. It has been corrected: both
attics span canonical z = -14 through 18, with the office kept inside the L.
The three rooms now share one rigid assembly transform, without independent
room fitting. Dimensions remain unmeasured; this does not certify exact scale.

The corrected common-edge image puts the office's east wall against the rack
attic's west wall. The earlier verbal specification puts that same office wall
against the existing house while also joining the rack attic's north wall to
the house. The currently assumed house footprint and floor levels cannot
satisfy all these contacts without overlap. The relative floor levels and
existing-house junction therefore remain unresolved. They are not repaired
by silently changing another room.
