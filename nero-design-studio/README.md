# Nero Design Studio

This is the ASC purple AI window, copied as it is.

`agent-card.css` and `agent-card.html` are that window: the aurora, the three hook lines, and the gradient send button. `star-field.css` is the faint drifting star field that already sits on it.

Future platforms copy these files. They do not get a second texture.

On ASC, registration keeps a bare number in the corner of the form. It has no frame. It shimmers purple, and pressing it asks the next plain question: the plate, the name, the phone, the email, the address, and whether the tires come back in six months.
